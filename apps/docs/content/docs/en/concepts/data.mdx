---
title: Databases, objects and records
description: Typed data, relations, computed fields, views and safety nets.
---

Data lives in three levels:

1. A **database** groups related objects (for example, "CRM").
2. An **object** is a schema of typed fields: text, number, email, select,
   date, relation, file, formula, lookup, rollup, autonumber and more. A field
   marked `unique` (such as an email) is enforced by the database and used as
   the upsert key.
3. A **record** is one row of an object.

## Writing

- `records.upsert` and `records.upsert_batch` create or update by unique field
  or `externalId`.
- `records.transact` writes linked records all or nothing.
- `records.merge` merges duplicates, relations and timeline included.
- `records.import_csv` imports a CSV, with deduplication.

## Reading

`records.search` filters, sorts and paginates (keyset pagination with `after`).
`query.sql` runs read-only SQL over the workspace. **Saved views** (filter,
sort, columns) are shared by the table, `records.search` and pages.

## Safety

Deleting moves records to the trash for 30 days (`records.restore`).
`records.history` and `records.revert` undo a change. Bulk operations by filter
require a `dryRun` preview first. Changing a schema that already has data goes
through `objects.migrate`.
